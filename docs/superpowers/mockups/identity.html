Name, logo and mood: which direction feels like your app?

Three directions. Each one shows the logo, the palette and a sketch of the main window with the big play button sitting half outside the window edge (the CleanMyMac idea). Names still need a trademark check.

Tributary

Every stream flows into one river.
Cool, focused, "night-shift coder" feel. Teal-to-violet glow. The logo is literally your sources merging into one stream.

Vesper

Music for the long evening session.
Warm, cozy, a bit vinyl-lounge. Sunset amber and pink. The logo is a crescent moon holding a small equalizer.

Prism

One player, every color of sound.
Neutral graphite glass and one spectral accent ring, which can take its color from the current album cover. Most "pro tool".
