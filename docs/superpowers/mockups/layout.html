What should the big half-out button do?

A

Play / Pause

The classic. Always the main transport control, and the spectral ring shows playback progress around the edge.

B

"Flow": one click, music starts

When nothing is playing, it builds an endless mix from your favorites across your music sources (local + Navidrome, later Apple Music), tuned for focus work. While playing, it turns into play/pause with the progress ring.
