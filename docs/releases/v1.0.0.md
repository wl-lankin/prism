# Prism 1.0.0

First release of Prism, a music player for your own music, your Navidrome server, internet radio and YouTube Music, for Windows and macOS.

**Library and formats**
- Add folders and Prism keeps the library up to date as files change.
- Plays MP3, AAC, ALAC, FLAC, WAV, AIFF, Ogg Vorbis, Opus, WMA, APE, WavPack, Musepack, TTA, DSD and MKA/WebM audio, with gapless albums and optional crossfade.
- Albums, artists, playlists, search, a play queue and an up next panel, plus repeat and shuffle.
- Covers from tags, folders, or looked up online when missing.

**Navidrome**
- Connect to your Navidrome server and browse and stream it next to your local files.
- Hearts sync with the server's stars both ways, its playlists show up, and plays are scrobbled.
- Your password is kept in the system keychain.

**Radio**
- Search internet radio stations worldwide, or see stations near you.
- Your location is only used roughly, and you can set a country and region by hand instead.
- Save stations, see the current song, and reconnect automatically after a dropout.

**YouTube Music**
- Sign in to YouTube Music in its own window and play it from within Prism.
- Prism never sees your Google password.
- The YouTube queue shows in Prism's queue panel, and media keys control it like any other source.

**Flow and favorites**
- Flow plays an endless mix from your favorites in your library and on Navidrome.
- Favorite songs, albums, artists and stations, all in one place.

**Mini player, tray and media keys**
- A small always-available mini player, optionally on top of other windows.
- Tray icon (Windows) and menu bar item (macOS) with playback controls.
- Media keys and the system media controls follow whichever source is playing; a global shortcut shows or hides the mini player.

**Languages**
- English and German.

**Updates**
- Prism checks for updates in the background and installs them when you quit or when you choose "Restart to update". Updates never interrupt what you are listening to.

## First install

The installers are not code-signed yet, so your system will warn you the first time. Code signing for Windows and notarization for macOS are planned for a later release.

**Windows**

Run `Prism-setup.exe`. If SmartScreen shows "Windows protected your PC", click "More info", then "Run anyway".

**macOS**

Open `Prism.dmg` and drag Prism to Applications. On first launch, right-click Prism and choose "Open", then confirm. Alternatively, remove the quarantine flag in Terminal:

```
xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Prism.app
```

Downloads: https://github.com/wl-lankin/prism/releases/latest/download/Prism-setup.exe and https://github.com/wl-lankin/prism/releases/latest/download/Prism.dmg
